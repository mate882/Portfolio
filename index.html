About Me

I’m a developer focused on building modern web experiences that combine responsive frontend development, interactive design, GSAP animation, and AI chatbots.

My current professional focus is frontend development. I build responsive websites using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with an emphasis on polished interfaces, smooth interactions, and strong user experience.

I also build custom AI chatbots and integrate them into websites, allowing visitors to interact with a business, product, service, or website content through a conversational interface.

Alongside my professional work, I’m expanding my software engineering and AI knowledge through hands-on projects and independent study. I prefer learning by building: implementing concepts, testing them, debugging problems, and improving the result.
